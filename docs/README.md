## Notes on this version

- Awesonix Mail connects to mail accounts with IMAP and SMTP. Use your password or an app password if your provider allows it. Sign-in with Google or Microsoft accounts (OAuth) is not available yet.
- The application is available in English, Hungarian, German, Dutch, French, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified), Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Spanish, and Italian. This documentation is in English only.
- The product owner reviewed the Hungarian texts. Independent reviews checked the German, Dutch, and French texts. The Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Spanish, and Italian texts are new. Agents translated and reviewed them. No native speaker has reviewed any of them yet.
